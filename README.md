A modern, role-based **Hotel Management System** built with **Python Flask**, **SQLAlchemy**, **SQLite**, **Flask-Login**, and **Bootstrap 5**.

The system is designed to manage the complete hotel operational workflow, including rooms, reservations, guests, check-in/check-out, housekeeping, payments, invoices, and management reports.

---

## 📌 Overview

The Hotel Management System provides a centralized platform for hotel staff and administrators to manage daily hotel operations.

The application supports:

- User authentication
- Role-based access control
- Guest management
- Room management
- Room status management
- Reservation management
- Booking confirmation
- Guest check-in
- Guest check-out
- Housekeeping management
- Invoice management
- Payment management
- Payment refunds
- Financial tracking
- Reports and analytics
- Revenue analysis
- Booking statistics
- Room occupancy monitoring
- Payment method analysis

The system follows a practical hotel workflow:
